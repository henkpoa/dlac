# AscensionXI Fishing — October 15, 2026

The Gear Helpers > Fishing Gear panel and the Hobby bar's Fishing tab are on for
AscensionXI. Branch `claude/axi-fishing` (dlac PR #215, `2026.10.15a`/`b`), paired with the
server branch `claude/dlac-fishing` (AscensionXI PR #964; its handoff:
`documentation/custom/dlac-fishing.md` in the AscensionXI repo). Offline suites
pass, including the client codec against the real server endpoint. **Not tried
in the client yet; the field round below is owed.** Not released.

Before this, the helper could not work on AscensionXI at all: the pack had no
`data/fishdb.lua` (the panel said "fishdb missing"), the helper was off in
`features.lua`, and everything the panel drew was CatsEye's (guild points,
Mariners venture gear, Crooked Jones).

## What the player gets

- **The panel**, AscensionXI's own layout (`fishui.renderShopPanel`):
  - the Set Fish Idle switch, the target window and the bar button;
  - **Move catches into: Mog Case / Mog Satchel**, per character, fish only;
    **Move trash too** (off by default) adds the non-fish catches;
  - the server's numbers: **Fishing points**, the skill in tenths with the
    best owned gear on top and the guild rank's cap, and the hook range ("Fish
    up to skill N can bite") with the skill-up chance at your skill;
  - **Fennella's shelf**: every Fisherman's piece (5,000 points) beside its
    Angler's upgrade (12,000 points and the piece), **the custom Fisherman's
    Cap and Angler's Cap included**; owned pieces green, a piece covered by its
    upgrade green with the "you're awesome" note, owned Angler's pieces lit;
    prices dim while you cannot afford them;
  - the rest of her shelf: the Carp Creel (12 Moat Carp a use), the Mog
    Wardrobe 3 slots ("2 of 5 bought"), the Fisher's Voidseal (owned or not);
  - other fishing gear you own that the shelf does not sell; rods; baits; the
    guild's next rank test (no guild-point shop: those points are never earned
    here).
- **The target window** follows this server's rules: a fish above your skill
  + 10 cannot bite ("cannot bite yet: needs skill N with gear"), skill-ups come
  at a flat chance until the fish's skill, your guild cap or 110 ("skill-ups:
  50% a catch, up to 40 (your guild cap -- then take the rank test)"), and each
  fish says what it pays in Fishing points. Bait isolation counts only rivals
  that can bite at your skill, so a water can be isolated at skill 20 and
  crowded at 60.
- **The hobby bar's Fishing tab**: points and skill, a warning while the
  target cannot bite yet, and the two move switches.
- **The idle set** wears the caps on its own: the catalog carries their
  FishingSkill, so the engine's `fish` ladder takes the Head slot like any
  other piece (Fisherman's +1, Angler's +2).

## What the server does (verified against AscensionXI main `18eeff4e67`)

- **Fishing points** bank on every landed fish, 40 to 80 by the fish's skill
  (`modules/custom/lua/fishing_points.lua`, charVar `[FISH]POINTS`); junk and
  monsters pay nothing. Fennella in Port Windurst sells the Fisherman's set and
  its cap for 5,000 each, upgrades a traded piece to its Angler's grade for
  12,000, and sells the creel (200), five wardrobe slots (20,000) and the
  Voidseal (10,000, after The Hollow Room).
- **Hooking**: with `FISHING_STATIC_PROGRESSION` on, a fish enters the hook
  pool only while its skill is at most your effective skill + 10
  (`fishing_progression_policy.h` `canHook`); effective = whole skill + worn
  Fishing skill.
- **Skill-ups**: +0.1 per landed fish (never items or monsters) at 50% below
  30, 40% below 60, 30% below 90, else 20%, while your base skill is below the
  fish's skill, your guild cap and 110.
- **The fail math** is CatsEye's, line for line (`fishingutils.cpp`
  CalculateLoseChance :795, Snap :860, Break :904), so the rod verdicts carry
  over unchanged.
- **A catch on the wire**: our 0x110 mode 3 (RequestEndMiniGame), then
  ITEM_ATTR + ITEM_SAME for the catch, then the bait's ITEM_SAME; our 0x110
  mode 4 (RequestRelease) follows the catch animation. The minigame is off, so
  every fight arrives already won.

## Op 0x83 and the packet budget

Same envelope and request as HELM's 0x80 (an 8-byte payload: u16 version 1,
u16 0, u32 token). The reply payload is 16 bytes (a 24-byte packet): the
echoed header, **u32 Fishing points, u16 skill in tenths, u8 wardrobe slots
bought, u8 guild rank**. Only the points and the tenths are server-only; the
rank fills the word, and with it the panel needs no `GetRank` read. The
decoder checks the declared wire size (24), version, token, skill <= 110.0 and
rank <= 10.

HELM and digging poll every five seconds while shown. Fishing asks only when
something can have changed the answer (the AXI packet budget): when a surface
first shows it (five seconds after a zone line), a second after each reel (a
landed fish banks its points before the reply leaves), a second after an event
option (0x05B: Fennella's shelf and her trade-up), once more when a request
was already out during a reel, and once a minute as a fallback while a surface
shows it. BUSY keeps the last snapshot and asks again in 1.5 s; UNAVAILABLE
clears it and waits 30 s; BAD_OP or an unsupported version stops asking until
the next zone or reload. Nothing is asked while no surface is open. An hour of
fishing with the bar up costs one request per catch plus one a minute.

## The fishing tables

`data/fishdb.lua` is the CatsEye shape (`docs/design/fishing-gear.md` §4) plus
three tables: `rules` (hookGate 10, skillCap 110, the gain bands),
`points` (the bands) and `shop` (Fennella's shelf). `fishdb.py` reads the
fishing SQL, the progression header, `fishing_points.lua` and the guild
master's test fish, and takes **the website's own era rules** straight out of
`website/tools/extract.ts` (POST_ERA_REGIONS, ERA_REGIONS, the unclassified
lists, SITE_HIDDEN_TAGS) rather than keeping a second copy: the wiki's fishing
pages and DLAC leave out the same waters. Cross-checked on October 15 against
the website's own baked `fishing.json`: all 165 waters and their fish are
identical. The table keeps only fish some water holds (126 of 128 enabled
rows) and drops gear above the level cap (Fisher's Torque, level 81). Item
names come from the client's item DATs.

## The catch mover

`feature/fishstorage.lua` is the third mover, built on HELM's: an owed count
per item and whole stacks only.

1. Our reel (0x110 mode 3) snapshots the inventory; the server's answer is
   counted once it has been quiet for 0.5 s, or at our release (mode 4). A reel
   never answered counts nothing.
2. Owed = the net rise of each item's total, but only for ids the fishing
   tables list as catches. A **fish** is always owed. **Trash** -- a row the
   server flags as not a fish (fishdb `item`: rusty gear, kelp, shells,
   scrolls) -- only while the player's **Move trash too** switch is on; it is
   off by default (Henrik, October 15: "I personally wouldn't want that").
   Switching it off forgets trash still waiting. Bait is never owed (the next
   cast needs it), nor is a broken rod.
3. Full stacks and non-stacking fish move as soon as the inventory is quiet;
   a partial stack once you have not reeled in for six seconds. With the
   13-second bite wait that is usually between casts, one move per catch.
4. Merges, zone lines, confirmations and stops are HELM's rules unchanged:
   a stack with room in either bag first, counts kept across a zone line,
   nothing moves while zoning, an unconfirmed move stops the mover until a
   switch is touched.

It arms only where the pack's `fishing` service says `moveCatches`, so it is
inert on CatsEye.

## Decisions made without the owner (say if any is wrong)

1. ~~"Move catches" moves everything the line brings up, junk included.~~
   **Ruled by Henrik on October 15 (`2026.10.15b`):** fish only; trash moves
   only with its own **Move trash too** switch, off by default. Bait and
   broken rods never move.
2. A partial stack moves six seconds after the last reel, which in practice
   means between casts.
3. Op 0x83 carries the skill in tenths, the wardrobe slots and the guild rank
   beside the points.
4. The points are asked for on reels, NPC menus and zone lines, with a
   one-minute fallback, instead of HELM's five-second poll.
5. The panel lists Fennella's whole shelf, as CatsEye's lists the whole
   Sinister Stash: the panel shows the balance, so it shows what it buys.
6. The fish search lists only fish some reachable water holds.
7. The guild corner keeps the rank test and drops the guild-point shop.

## Field round (owner's client)

1. Open Gear Helpers > Fishing Gear: points, skill (with tenths) and rank
   appear within a second or two. Land a fish: the points rise about a second
   later. Buy or upgrade at Fennella: the numbers update after her menu.
2. Zone: the numbers clear and come back about five seconds later with the
   panel open.
3. The shelf: owned pieces green, both caps listed with icons and prices,
   "N of 5 bought", the Voidseal marked owned when you have it.
4. Target window: a fish more than ten above your skill with gear says it
   cannot bite yet; a fish above your skill shows the skill-up line; every
   fish shows its points.
5. Set Fish Idle with the caps in your bags: the best cap goes on the head.
6. Tick Mog Case: a non-stacking fish leaves the inventory at once, a
   stacking one about six seconds after the reel; trash (a rusty bucket, kelp)
   stays; bait stays; a broken rod stays; nothing moves while zoning. Tick
   Move trash too: the next trash you reel in moves as well; untick it and
   trash caught meanwhile stays.
7. On a server without op 0x83: the panel says "waiting for the server", the
   shelf still draws, no errors.
